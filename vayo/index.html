The idea

We don't sell trips.

We sell stories.

For a few days, the normal rules disappear. No work. No errands. No deciding what happens next. You wake somewhere unfamiliar, meet people you would never normally meet, eat differently, move differently — and see what happens.

We design the circumstances. Our guides hold the thread. You live the story.

The VAYO philosophy

Highly designed backstage.
Genuinely organic frontstage.

A beautiful place is not enough. A list of activities is not enough. We create the conditions for surprise, connection, freedom and the kind of moments that become stories.

How it works

Booking should feel easier than planning the trip yourself.

Pick a story. Tell us who is coming. We take care of the details. Payment is the final step — not the beginning of a maze.

01 / Choose your story

Explore an experience and open its story page. See the mood, pace, people, stay and what might happen.

02 / Check the fit

Select your dates, group size and pace. A VAYO host can call if the experience needs a little conversation first.

03 / Make it yours

Tell us dietary needs, preferences and the one thing you'd love to experience. We adapt the edges, not the soul.

04 / Confirm

Review the experience, inclusions, cancellation terms and total. Nothing important is hidden.

05 / Payment

Secure checkout would happen here in the live product. This prototype deliberately stops before collecting real card details.
